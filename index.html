About Me

After graduating, I began my teaching career at Da Vinci Senior Secondary School, where I taught Mathematics and General Science while guiding and supporting students throughout their academic journey. I later taught Biology and General Science at Achievers Training Center for two years, helping students who required a pass in Biology in the West African Senior School Certificate Examination to pursue nursing and other health-related programs. I also taught Mathematics and General Science at Khalid Bun Waleed Islamic School, where I worked with students from diverse educational backgrounds and age groups. While teaching, I pursued a degree in Computer Science, which introduced me to the field of technology and developed my interest in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. With the support of my professors, I completed my thesis titled *“An Artificial Intelligence Approach to Preventing and Predicting Heart Attacks in The Gambia Using Machine Learning Algorithms,”* which was approved and archived for future research and publication. Currently, I work at the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority as an Information and Communication Technology Assistant, where I continue to strengthen my technical and professional skills.
